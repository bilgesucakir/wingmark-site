Your personal bird diary

Log the birds you see, learn the species, hear their calls.

Whether you’ve watched birds for years or only just started noticing the ones outside your window.

Coming soon on the App Store

Log it in a few taps

The bird, the date, the place, a note and a photo. Say whether it was male or female, young or adult, or a pet.

Not sure what you saw? Save it as a guess, or tell Wingmark you don’t know the species yet.

Remember where you saw it

Every sighting is pinned on the map with its photo. Tap a cluster to open the birds seen in that spot.

Filter the map by identification, sex and life stage to find what you’re after.

Get to know the species

The built-in guide covers 570+ species. Narrow it by order and family, or search in English or Turkish.

Each bird has photos, its size, what it eats, where it lives and how rare it is.

Learn birds by ear

Play real field recordings of calls and songs, from xeno-canto, right on each species page.

Next time you hear something in the trees, you’ll know where to start.

One tidy diary

All your sightings in a single list, each with its photo, date, place and notes.

Open any entry to read it again, edit it, or add a new one in a moment.

Earn badges

Collect milestones as your sightings and species grow. Some of them are secret.

Wingmark on your Home Screen

A new Bird of the Day from the guide every day. Widgets also show your latest sighting and the badge you’re closest to earning.

Yours alone

  • Only you can see your sightings, notes and photos.
  • No ads and no tracking.
  • Download your data or delete your account any time in Settings.
  • Available in English and Turkish.
